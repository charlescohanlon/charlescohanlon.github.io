I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at [NYU's Courant Institute](https://cs.nyu.edu/). I received my B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics from Cal Poly San Luis Obispo.

I'm broadly interested in exploring **inductive biases for sample-efficient multimodal learning**, **semantic embedding spaces**, and **world models as learned representations for planning and control**.

Currently, I'm at Blue Origin working on a meaningful semantic representation space for an internal multimodal datatype.

Previously, I was a Research Assistant at **Argonne National Laboratory** (UChicago/DOE), where I studied Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks as architectural priors for implicit neural representations of scientific data volumes.

In industry, I interned at **LandingAI** (founded by Dr. Andrew Ng), where I worked on VLM fine-tuning, attention diagnostics, and large-scale data distillation pipelines. I also worked at **femtoAI** 

At Cal poly, I worked on action segmentation from video (funded by the National Cancer Institute) with **Ventura Lab**, and I attempted to build multimodal location predictors for missing person cases with the **AI for Search & Rescue Lab**.
