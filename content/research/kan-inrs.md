title: KAN-Based Implicit Neural Representations
description: Studied Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks as an alternative architectural prior for implicit neural representations of volumetric scientific data at Argonne National Laboratory. Achieved +2–15 dB PSNR over SIREN/MLP baselines and characterized regimes where learned activation functions outperform fixed-activation INRs via systematic ablation. Trained on the ALCF Sophia DGX A100 cluster.
